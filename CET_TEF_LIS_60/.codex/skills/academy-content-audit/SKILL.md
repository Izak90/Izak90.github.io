# Workflow
Read docs/CONTENT_RULES.md and docs/QUESTION_BANKS.md.
Extract each saber bullet/sub-bullet. Map to summary location and question IDs. Mark explicit/implicit/missing. Replace redundant content/questions to close gaps. Do not claim 100% until every item is explicit. Do not fill source gaps silently.
